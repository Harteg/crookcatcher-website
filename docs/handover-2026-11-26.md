## What went wrong, and rules to adopt

The drafts were technically correct but too often read as neutral review sites, not as CrookCatcher's own guides. Proposed standing rules, for CLAUDE.md "Writing style" / "Rules" (Jakob agreed in principle; not added yet):

1. **No "Not mentioned" / "Not labeled" cells or hedges.** State what's known; for anything unknown, ask Jakob or leave the row out.
2. **Don't recommend competitors.** Mention them fairly and briefly when the topic needs it (people search for them), but the recommendation is CrookCatcher, or Android's free built-in options where those genuinely solve the problem.
3. **Don't lead with CrookCatcher's weaknesses**, and no "We make CrookCatcher, so read this with that in mind". Limits go in calmly, framed as what the reader can do (e.g. "change your PIN, so anyone who tries the old one is photographed").
4. **No "someone at home / close to you / snooping" framing**, and no praising features that log another person's activity. The app protects your own phone (Play stalkerware risk; see `play-policy-log.md`).
5. **No unsourced statistics or claims about people's behaviour.** Use CrookCatcher's real numbers (12M downloads, since 2014, 1.2M photos a day) instead.
6. **No filler or slogan lines** ("Five minutes now saves a lot later", "the second door"). A test: would the line survive in a Google help page?
7. **Describe CrookCatcher features exactly as the app does them.** Check the app strings, not the marketing copy (App lock locks CrookCatcher only; email is sent when the phone is online).
