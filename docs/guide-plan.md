# CrookCatcher Guide Plan

Oct 21, 2026 · @Jakob

Publish about 30 genuinely useful guides on crookcatcher.app over the next 6 weeks, so Google sends Android users searching for intruder, unlock-attempt and theft problems to CrookCatcher.

## Goal and audience

- **Reader:** an Android user, mainly in the US, who worries someone is trying to get into their phone, or wants protection before a theft.
- **Best searches:** pre-theft and curiosity searches ("who tried to unlock my phone"). Someone whose phone is already gone can't install the app anymore, so post-theft guides are for goodwill and next-phone installs.
- **Platform filter:** every title says Android, Samsung, Pixel or Xiaomi. iPhone users can't install the app.
- **Success after 8 weeks:** guides appear in Search Console for real queries, and Play Console shows installs with `utm_source=website`, broken down per guide.

## Publishing rhythm

Write in batches with Claude Code once or twice a week, then publish about one guide a day. Never publish a guide you haven't read.

1. **Weeks 1–3:** publish Priority 1 and Priority 2 guides (about 15).
2. **Weeks 3–6:** publish scenario and comparison pages.
3. **Week 6 onward:** let Search Console data choose new topics, and consider automating the "find the gap, write the page" loop.

Weekly 10-minute check:

- [ ] Search Console: which queries show impressions? Add new topics to the tables below.
- [ ] Play Console acquisition report: which guides (`utm_campaign`) produced installs?
- [ ] Add internal links from older guides and the homepage to the newest guides.
- [ ] Request indexing in Search Console for new guides.

## Guide template

Every guide follows the same structure, adapted from Vaultaire's guides.

1. **Title** with the device name and year where it fits: "How to See Who Tried to Unlock Your Samsung Phone (2026)".
2. **Answer first:** the opening paragraph fully answers the question on its own (this is what Google's AI overviews quote).
3. **Honest built-in options:** what Android or Samsung can already do, with real steps.
4. **The gap:** what the built-in options can't do (usually: no photo of the person, no email alert).
5. **How CrookCatcher fills it:** short, with a screenshot of the intruder photo or email alert.
6. **FAQ:** 4–6 questions people actually search, marked up with FAQ schema.
7. **Sources:** links to Google or Samsung support pages used.
8. **Related guides:** 2–3 internal links.
9. **One CTA at the end** plus the existing header button, each Play link tagged `utm_source=website&utm_medium=guide&utm_campaign=<guide-slug>`.

Where possible, add something only CrookCatcher has: real numbers (12M downloads, 1.2M intruder photos a day) or a real user story (with permission).

## Rules

- No mass publishing: never more than a few guides per day.
- No unreviewed AI text: read every guide before it goes live.
- No drifting to unrelated topics; stay close to "someone has my phone or tried to get into it."
- No framing that sounds like spying on a partner or another person's phone (Play policy risk).
- No real intruder faces in screenshots; use your own photo or blur it.
- No user stories or review quotes without the person's permission.
- No iPhone-targeted guides; the app is Android only.
